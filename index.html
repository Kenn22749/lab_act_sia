Nissan R34 GTR

The Nissan Skyline R34 GTR is one of the most iconic JDM cars ever made. Powered by the legendary RB26DETT engine, it became famous in racing and movies like Fast & Furious.

Mazda RX-7

The Mazda RX-7 is famous for its lightweight body and rotary engine. It became a drifting legend and a fan favorite in JDM culture.

Honda NSX

The Honda NSX is Japan's first supercar. Designed with Formula 1 technology and even tested by Ayrton Senna.

Mitsubishi Evo X

The Mitsubishi Lancer Evolution X is known for its aggressive design and rally heritage. It is one of the most powerful AWD JDM performance cars.
