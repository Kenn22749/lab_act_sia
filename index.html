The Evolution of Godzilla

R32 GT-R

Produced from 1989-1994, the R32 earned the nickname "Godzilla" because it dominated racing in Japan and Australia with its RB26DETT twin-turbo engine.

R33 GT-R

Released in 1995, the R33 improved stability and aerodynamics. It became the first production car to lap the Nürburgring under 8 minutes.

R34 GT-R

Produced from 1999-2002, the R34 became the most iconic GTR thanks to its appearance in racing, gaming, and movies like Fast & Furious.

R35 GT-R

Released in 2007, the R35 dropped the Skyline name and introduced a modern twin-turbo V6 with advanced AWD technology.
